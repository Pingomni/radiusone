# RadiusOne 2.19.0

A new top for the OLT's page: its front panel as it stands in the rack. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*,
or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **The front panel.** Every slot from the first card to the last, empty slots behind a blanking plate, each card with its
  code, its RUN or PWR light, its temperature, CPU and power use, and a cage with an LED for each port. A PON port with
  customers shows how many are online on it (11/16). The fans' speed is along the bottom.
- **Choose a PON port** on the panel to list that port's ONUs below, without loading the page again. Choose it again, or
  the **Port ×** chip, to list every ONU.
- **One column for the customers:** how many ONUs are online, then **Offline**, **Weak signal** and **Uplinks up**. Each
  leads to what it counts. **Needs a look** lists what to check, worst first.
- **The OLT's details at a glance:** model, address, software, uptime, power use, the hottest card and whether changes are
  allowed, each with its label.
- **Tabs in groups:** the ONUs; the OLT's details; its ports, cards and graphs; its VLANs and profiles; and its backups,
  terminal and changelog.
- **On a phone** the panel puts each card's ports under its name, so it fits without scrolling sideways.

## What the update changes on the server

Nothing besides the code. Customers stay connected.
