# RadiusOne 2.33.1

Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

One design across every page:

- **No more coloured pills:** the OLT and ONU pages (port and card states, autofind, rogue ONU, ONU status and
  signal, the OLT's problem list), the Settings section headers and the remaining spots now show a dot and a word.
- **Sub-tabs are underlined everywhere:** Settings → Notifications (Templates, Gateways, History, Admin alerts),
  Logs (All, Rejects, Accepts) and a NAS's network tables. Time-range pickers keep their boxed buttons.
- **No icons in panel titles** (Settings, My account, client sections). Tab strips show icons only on screens wider
  than 1680 px.
- **OLT Ports tab** fits a 1440 px screen: free places sit under the ONU count, and In and Out share a column.
- Settings → Users: no empty row for an export button. The "noc" role reads "NOC".

## What the update changes on the server

Nothing besides the code. Customers stay connected.
