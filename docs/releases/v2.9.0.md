# RadiusOne 2.9.0

Customer contracts, and a pay page where customers pay their internet by card with POK or Stripe. Update with
*Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **Contracts.** Issue a contract for a customer's plan, term and billing period from your own template (Settings →
  Contracts), print it as a PDF, and follow its invoices. The text is kept as issued. Nightly invoices follow the contract.
- **A pay page on its own address**, such as `pay.example.com`, in Albanian or English, with your logo and colour. The
  customer enters their username and the last 4 digits of their phone number, picks 1, 3, 6 or 12 months inside the
  contract, and pays on POK's or Stripe's own page. Card details never reach RadiusOne.
- **Paid means paid.** A payment counts only after RadiusOne asks POK or Stripe and finds it complete for the full amount.
  Then the invoice is issued and paid and the service renewed at once. Anything else waits for a person under **Online
  payments**, and admin alerts mention it.
- **Test and live.** Each service has test and live credentials, saved apart, and a switch for which one the page uses.
  It starts in test mode. A test payment is recorded, but never issues an invoice or renews a service.
- **Online payment per client.** A client's page has **May pay on the pay page**, on by default.
- **Guessing is stopped.** After 5 wrong phone numbers, a username waits 15 minutes.

Contracts and the pay page are RadiusOne Pro features. Setting it up: [pingomni.com/docs/radiusone](https://pingomni.com/docs/radiusone/#12-pay-page-and-online-payments).

## What the update changes on the server

Two new database tables (contracts, online payments) and one new column on clients. A new job checks open payments every
5 minutes. Nothing charges until you add keys and turn the page on. Customers stay connected.
