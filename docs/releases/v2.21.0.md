# RadiusOne 2.21.0

The portal's new frame. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **The page is one light sheet in a dark frame,** with the menu on the frame.
- **The menu in four groups:** Operations, Network, Finance and Workspace. Clients shows how many there are; OLT and ONU
  shows how many ONUs have a weak signal.
- **Search from the top bar:** Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac) jumps to it. Clients by name, username, phone, IP or MAC, and
  invoices by number, appear as you type.
- **A breadcrumb** in the top bar and the page title on the page.
- **Your account, the theme switch and sign out** are at the bottom of the menu.

## What the update changes on the server

Nothing besides the code. Customers stay connected.
