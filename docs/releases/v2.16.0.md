# RadiusOne 2.16.0

A live console on the OLT, a new OLT page, and a faster ONU page. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

## Terminal: a live console on the OLT

- **A real terminal, as with PuTTY,** on the OLT's **Terminal** tab: one telnet session stays open while you work, every
  key goes to the OLT as you type it, and its answers come back as they arrive. So `?` lists what can follow, **Tab**
  completes, **↑** brings back the last commands, **Space** shows the next page of a long answer and **Ctrl+C** stops
  a command, all as on the OLT itself. Colours and line redraws show as the OLT sends them.
- **The portal logs in for you,** with the telnet user saved for the OLT: the browser never sees that user or password.
  From an ONU's page, **Terminal** opens the console already in that ONU's `interface gpon`.
- **Tools beside it:** common display commands one click away, More, Ctrl+C, text size, full screen, Disconnect and
  Reconnect. **Ctrl+Shift+C** copies the selection.
- **Who:** operators who may delete, on an OLT with *Allow changes from the portal* on. Others keep the read-only command
  line. Each line typed is written to the audit log under the operator's name, with what follows a password, key or
  community masked; a line finished with Tab or recalled with ↑ is marked "…" where the OLT completed it.
- **Limits:** two console sessions per OLT (the portal's own reads need a telnet login too) and ten per server. A session
  closes after 10 minutes without use, and after 2 hours.
- **How it works:** a new service, **radiusone-olt-console**, holds the sessions. The update installs and starts it. It
  listens only on a local socket the portal can reach; keys and output pass through the portal's own pages, so nothing
  changes in nginx or in a proxy in front of the portal. If the service is not running, the Terminal tab says so and
  falls back to sending one line at a time, with careful commands confirmed first.
- **The portal's security policy** now lets pages add their own `<style>` elements, which the terminal needs to lay out
  its rows. Style attributes and scripts stay as strict as before.

## The OLT's page

- **At a glance:** a header like the ONU's, coloured by the OLT's health, with what needs a look spelled out: a failed read,
  an uplink down, a card or fan fault, a PON port with every ONU offline, or an OLT that does not save its configuration.
  Model, address, software, uptime, power and temperature beside it, and Read now, New ONUs, Terminal and Edit.
- **The chassis:** each card in its slot with its state and role, and a light for each port: up, some ONUs offline, down,
  no ONUs, turned off, or no module. Hover a light for its ONUs and signal.
- **ONUs online, offline, weak signal and uplinks** as tiles; **Offline** opens the list of offline ONUs.
- **PON ports in use:** each port with ONUs, how many are online, and their average signal.
- **The ONU list** shows each ONU's name with its serial number, both signals together, and All, Online and Offline with
  their counts.
- **An uplink port with no module fitted** counts as spare, not as down.

## A faster ONU page

- **One login to the OLT per page** instead of up to three: the page's read also brings the ONU's running configuration,
  so the **Configure** and **Running config** tabs open without asking the OLT again.
- **Location and Customer** now share a column beside Device and Service, and the 24-hour strip shows 00:00, not 24:00.
- **A read serves for 30 seconds:** a reload, a tab or a second person on the same ONU uses it, and the page says how old it
  is. **Get status**, **Live** and **Read again** always ask the OLT. A change on the ONU makes the next read new.
- **The OLT's VLANs, profiles and speeds** are read once every 10 minutes for the Configure tab, and again at once after
  **Add a speed**.

## What to know

- **More people at once:** each read of the OLT keeps one of the server's PHP workers busy for its few seconds. A default
  PHP-FPM pool has 5. If several people work on ONU pages at the same time, give the pool more: set
  `pm.max_children = 20` in `/etc/php/8.3/fpm/pool.d/www.conf` and run `sudo systemctl reload php8.3-fpm`.

## What the update changes on the server

A new service, `radiusone-olt-console`, is installed and started. Nothing in the database. No OLT is changed by the
update. Customers stay connected.
