# RadiusOne 2.13.0

A full page for each ONU, with its history, speed profiles and LAN ports, and more of the OLT's own settings. Update with
*Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

## The ONU's page

- **Traffic history and signal history.** Two graphs per ONU, over 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days, and a count of how often
  the ONU went offline in the last 24 hours and 7 days.
- **Right now.** State and how long it has been online, both signals, traffic at this moment, routing or bridging, its WAN
  address, the VLANs attached, software and temperatures. **Get status** asks again, and **Live** keeps asking every 10 seconds.
- **Show running config.** The ONU's part of the OLT's configuration, as the OLT has it.
- **Speed profiles.** Each service port with its VLAN and its download and upload ceiling. **Configure** moves it to other
  traffic tables of the OLT.
- **Ethernet ports.** Each LAN port's state, link, speed and native VLAN, with **Turn off** and **Turn on**.
- **Restore defaults.** Puts the ONU back to its factory settings. With **Reboot**, **Disable ONU** and **Delete**.
- **The client's name, address and phone** show on the ONU's page when the ONU is linked to a client.

## The OLT

- **Signal levels.** Under **Edit**, set from which signal an ONU counts as weak and as very weak, per OLT.
- **Time servers.** Add and remove the OLT's NTP servers on the **OLT details** tab.
- **VLANs.** Create and delete VLANs, and add a VLAN to an uplink port or take it off, on the **VLANs and profiles** tab.

## What to know

- **ONU settings go through OMCI:** the portal changes them on the OLT, and the OLT pushes them to the ONU.
- **The graphs start empty.** A point is added every 5 minutes from the update on, and kept for 8 days. Traffic comes from
  the OLT's own counters per service port, so it needs no login to the ONU.
- **The portal protects the OLT's own VLAN.** It will not delete the VLAN the OLT has its management address on, or take it
  off an uplink port. It will not delete a VLAN that ONUs or uplink ports still use.
- **Every change needs Allow changes from the portal,** checks the ONU's serial number on the OLT first, and is written to
  the audit log with the commands sent. Removing and restoring defaults need the right to delete.
- **Checked on a Huawei MA5800-X7** (V100R018C00): everything that reads was run against a real OLT. The changes were
  tested on a simulator. **Restore defaults** uses a command that was not confirmed on a real OLT: if the OLT does not
  know it, you see the OLT's error and nothing is changed.
- **Not here:** Wi-Fi name and password, VoIP, moving an ONU to another port, and link aggregation on uplinks.

## What the update changes on the server

One new table (ONU history) and five new columns. No OLT is changed by the update. Customers stay connected.
