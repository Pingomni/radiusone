# RadiusOne 2.8.0

An OLT's own hardware, on the OLT's page and on Monitoring. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **Ports.** Each uplink and PON port's state, speed, traffic and errors, and the ONUs online on each PON port.
- **Cards.** Each card's status, role, CPU, memory, temperature and power input, and fans if the OLT reports them.
- **Graphs.** Control card CPU and memory, the hottest card, and uplink traffic, over 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days.
- **Monitoring.** The OLT's card shows CPU, memory, temperature, cards and ports. **Needs attention** lists a faulty card, a power or
  fan fault, a card at 75 °C or more, a card's CPU at 90 % or more, and an uplink that's down.

## What to know

- **Read every minute,** over SNMP only. Traffic appears from the second read, because it's the change between two reads.
- **Fans show only if the OLT reports them.** Huawei's MIBs have fan objects, but not every model fills them in. If this OLT
  doesn't, the page says so.
- **CPU and memory are on control cards only.** That's what the MIB defines. Other cards show a dash.
- **A card being found or set up, or turned off by hand, isn't an alert.**
- **A PON port that's down is not an alert,** because that's normal when nothing is plugged in. PON outages still show when
  the ONUs on a port go offline.
- **Not yet checked against a real OLT.** The values come from Huawei's MIBs, and were tested against a simulator. A dash means
  the OLT didn't report that value.

## What the update changes on the server

Four new database tables and two new columns. Customers stay connected.
