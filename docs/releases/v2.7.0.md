# RadiusOne 2.7.0

OLTs on the Monitoring page. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **A card for each OLT.** Shows its ONUs online, offline and with a weak signal, and how old the list is.
- **Needs attention.** Lists a failed read, an OLT no longer being read, and a PON port whose ONUs all went offline.
- **A graph on the OLT's page.** ONUs online, offline and with a weak signal, over 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days.
- **Background jobs.** Shows the OLT reader once an OLT is added. **Poll all devices now** also reads the OLTs, within a minute.

## What to know

- **Everything comes from the last read,** every 5 minutes, so the Monitoring page never contacts an OLT. A status can be up to
  5 minutes old, and the card says how old the list is.
- **What counts as a PON outage:** two or more ONUs on the port, all offline, and at least one seen online in the last 24 hours.
  A port with a single ONU doesn't count, and a port that stays down leaves the list after a day.
- **No email or SMS alerts for OLTs yet.** These problems show on the Monitoring page only.
- **The graph starts empty.** It fills from the first read after the update.

## What the update changes on the server

One new database table and one new column. Customers stay connected.
