# RadiusOne 2.11.0

More of the OLT on its page: what it is and how it is doing, each port's optics and VLANs, its VLANs and profiles, copies of
its configuration, and a history of what was done. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **OLT details.** A new first tab: model, software and patch, how long the OLT has been running, power use in watts, the
  hottest card, fan speeds, its time servers and whether it is in sync with them, and whether it saves its configuration by itself.
- **Fans in RPM, power per card.** The **Cards** tab shows each fan's speed and each card's power use.
- **PON ports.** Each port also shows autofind on or off, the average signal of its online ONUs, the transmit power and
  temperature of its optical module, its range, and a warning if the OLT reports a rogue ONU.
- **Uplink ports.** Each port also shows its type (GE, 10GE), whether a module is fitted, and the VLANs it carries.
- **VLANs and profiles.** A new tab lists the OLT's VLANs, line and service profiles, traffic tables and DBA profiles, with
  how many ONUs use each.
- **Config backups.** The portal reads the OLT's configuration once a day and keeps a copy each time it changed, up to 30.
  **Copy now** takes one at once. Copies are stored sealed, and each download is written to the audit log.
- **History.** A new tab lists what was done on this OLT from the portal, who did it, and the commands sent.
- **Export MAC table.** Downloads every MAC behind an ONU, with the ONU and its client, as a CSV file.

## What to know

- **All of this only reads the OLT.** Nothing here changes it, and it works with **Allow changes from the portal** off.
- **Fans, power and uptime come over SNMP,** every minute. The rest comes over telnet every 15 minutes, with the MAC table,
  so it needs telnet turned on for the OLT. A dash means the OLT did not report that value.
- **A configuration copy holds the OLT's own passwords.** Only people who may manage NAS devices see and download copies.
- **Not here yet:** changing ports, uplinks, VLANs or profiles from the portal, restarting a card, and SNMP traps.
- **Checked against a real Huawei MA5800-X7** (V100R018C00): every value above was read from it.

## What the update changes on the server

One new table (configuration copies) and nine new columns. No OLT is changed by the update. Customers stay connected.
