# RadiusOne 2.18.0

A changelog for each OLT, and a history for each ONU. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

## The OLT's changelog

- **The Changelog tab** (in place of History) lists everything that changed on the OLT: when, who, what, which ONU or port,
  and the commands the OLT got, opened with a click. Each ONU links to its page.
- **Changes made on the OLT itself are in it too.** The portal compares each read with the one before: an ONU added,
  removed, moved to another port, renamed, turned off or on, or given another profile on the OLT, from its command line or
  by another system such as SmartOLT, shows as **On the OLT**.
- **The configuration's changes, line by line.** When the daily copy of the configuration differs from the one before, the
  changelog shows the lines added and removed. The OLT's passwords and communities in them are masked.
- **Commands typed in the terminal** that change something are in it. Commands that only read are left out.
- **Search, and filter** by what (ONUs, PON ports and cards, VLANs and speeds, OLT settings, configuration, terminal), by
  where it came from (the portal, the OLT, the terminal) and by who. **Export** gives the list as a CSV file.
- **What was already in the audit log** about the OLT and its ONUs is copied in by the update, so the changelog starts
  with the history you have.

## The ONU's history

- **A History tab** on each ONU's page: what was done to that ONU, from being added to now, and whom it was linked to.
- **Added to the OLT** on the ONU's page shows when the ONU was added, when the portal knows.

## What to know

- **Kept for two years.**
- **Changes made on the OLT are found at the next read,** within 5 minutes for ONUs and within a day for the configuration.
  The changelog says when the portal found them, not when they were made.
- **Who made a change on the OLT** is not known to the portal: such a change shows as **On the OLT**.

## What the update changes on the server

One new table (the changelog), filled from the audit log by the update. Customers stay connected.
