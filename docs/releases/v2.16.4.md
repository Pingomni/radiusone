# RadiusOne 2.16.4

A fix for the ONU's page. Update with *Settings → Overview → Software update*, or `sudo radiusone-update`.

- **Choosing a section showed only its first lines.** Clicking Graphs, Ports, Speed profiles, Configure, Running config
  or Manage left the section at the bottom of the window, so you had to scroll to see it. The section now moves to the
  top of the window when you choose it, and the whole section shows.
- **Going from a long section to a short one no longer moves the page.**
- **The row of sections no longer shows a scroll bar of its own.**

## What the update changes on the server

Nothing besides the code. Customers stay connected.
